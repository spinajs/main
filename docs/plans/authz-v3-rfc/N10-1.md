# RFC N10-1: run ExternalLoginHooks inside the core flow, before the session

Status: PROPOSED (non-blocking; N10 ships a workaround).

Lane N10 (authn-oidc), final lane review, finding I2. Affected: `DefaultAuthnService` (authn core, N1 code,
not frozen) and the doc of `ExternalLoginHook` in `packages/authn/src/contracts/hooks.ts` (frozen: a doc
change needs this RFC).

## Problem

Design §22.15 says a throwing `ExternalLoginHook` fails the login. The core never calls the hooks, so
authn-oidc calls them after `AuthnService.authenticate` returned. By then the core has issued the session
and audited `login` success and `session.issue`. When a hook throws, authn-oidc revokes the session and
publishes its own `login` failure (reason `hook_error`), so the audit trail holds a success, then a
failure, for one attempt; a session existed for a moment; and every external strategy (LDAP and SAML
later) must repeat the same fan-out and clean-up.

## Change

1. `DefaultAuthnService.authenticate`, for an outcome with `external`: after the identity is resolved
   (link, verified email or JIT) and its status checked, and before `throttle.succeed`, MFA and the
   session, call every `Array.ofType(ExternalLoginHook)` with `(identity, external)`. A throwing hook
   ends the attempt as `invalid_credentials`, audited with reason `hook_error`; no session is issued.
2. The doc of `ExternalLoginHook` says the core calls it, before the session, and that a throwing hook
   fails the login.
3. authn-oidc then drops its own fan-out, the strategy's verified-subject hand-off and the revoke path.

## Affected lanes

N10 (removes its workaround), N11/N12 later (no work of their own), C3 if `users` registers a hook.
