Breaking for subclasses of `StompQueueClient`: `handleUnparseableMessage(message, desc, err)` takes the subscription descriptor where it took the channel name; `deadLetter(message, dlq, headers)` takes the header map where it took the channel, the reason and the attempt; `retryBackoff(attempt)` is removed (set the routing `backoff`). The signature of `handleFailedMessage(message, qMessage, desc, err)` is unchanged. `executeDecision`, `consumerQueueOf`, `assertSubscribable`, `endSubscription` and `readVirtualTopics` are new.

Failures are decided by `@spinajs/queue`'s `decideDelivery`. Behaviour changes under defaults: `NonRetryableError` dead-letters at once; retry copies carry `x-error`, dead letters keep `x-original-destination` and the frame's `x-spinajs-message-id`; the `x-error` of an unparseable body starts with `unparseable message body:`; a `crash: true` error is logged and leaves the frame unsettled; a `RedeliverLater` on a `/queue/…` channel is republished into that channel with its delay, for events too. An event consumed from a `/queue/…` channel is retried into that channel when its route says `onEventFailure: 'retry'`. New `options.virtualTopics` (off): a named subscription of `/topic/VirtualTopic.X` consumes `/queue/Consumer.<subscriptionId>.VirtualTopic.X`, and retries, event retries (`onEventFailure: 'retry'`) and `RedeliverLater` included, go to that queue only. With it, a named topic subscription must be on a `/topic/VirtualTopic.*` channel and its `subscriptionId` cannot contain `.`, `*` or `>`. `onEventFailure: 'retry'` on a topic subscription without a consumer queue throws from `subscribe()`, so `consume()` fails at boot. `RedeliversFailedEvents` is `options.virtualTopics`, so `queue.deduplicateEvents` accepts a STOMP connection only with virtual topics; a `RedeliverLater` on a topic subscription without a consumer queue is nacked, never acked.
