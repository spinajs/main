`TableAuditStore` implements `joinsCaller`: a confirmed write made while a transaction is open on its connection, on a driver that serves one transaction at a time (`supportedFeatures().concurrentTransactions` false: sqlite, PGlite), is written inside that transaction instead of waiting for it to end until `audit.confirmTimeoutMs`. There it commits and rolls back with the caller's transaction; it is tried once, without a deadline, under a savepoint, so a failed insert leaves the caller's transaction usable. Joined writes of one transaction run one after another, so parallel `record` calls cannot interleave their savepoints. A failure of the savepoint itself is reported as `audit: recording <n> event(s) of trail "<t>" on connection "<c>" failed at its savepoint in the caller's transaction`, with `; that transaction may be unusable` when it failed after the insert had started. On pooled drivers (Postgres, MySQL, MSSQL) a confirmed write stays detached and is kept when the caller's transaction rolls back.
