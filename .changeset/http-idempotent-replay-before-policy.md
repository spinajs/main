Behaviour change: `@Idempotent` replays a completed key of an authenticated, unrestricted principal before the policy gate, on routes with policies, when the request has the stored fingerprint, so the resend of a committed operation (a delete whose row is gone, a state change a policy now refuses) gets its stored reply instead of a 403 or 404. The policies and the action do not run for it; the reply goes through the reply pipeline, where the server middlewares and the route's `'beforePolicy'` middlewares (`Audit` included) see it in `onAfter`, while the `'outer'` and `'inner'` route middlewares are not entered. This is the single exception to "the policies always run after the `'beforePolicy'` middlewares": the replay needs the same principal identity and authority and the same request fingerprint, and nothing executes. Policies that do not depend on the principal (IP allowlist, MFA step-up, tenant header) are not evaluated again on such a replay, and a principal whose permission was revoked within `ttl` still gets the reply it was owed. Anonymous callers (one shared scope), restricted principals (a `ceiling` from a scoped token, signing key or role switch, `onBehalfOf`, `authn.impersonator`, a share link, `companions`), keys in flight, reused keys, records without a fingerprint and a failing store (logged) go through the policies and the claim as before; 401 and 403 are still never stored.

Behaviour change: the key scope of a restricted principal holds its authority as well as its `type:id`, so a reply stored for a user's full session never replays to a scoped token, a role switch or an impersonating admin of that user, nor the other way round. Unrestricted principals keep their scope. A key a restricted credential completed before the upgrade runs again when resent.

Behaviour change: in a shared scope (an anonymous caller, or a share link bearer without a signed-in companion, who share one key space with every caller like them) `@Idempotent` takes only a random UUID, version 4 or 7, as the `Idempotency-Key`, and refuses any other key with 400 `idempotency.key-not-random`, so one caller's key can neither collide with nor guess another's. The check runs after the policies (401 and 403 still win) and before the claim, so a refused key runs nothing and claims nothing. A request without the header is unaffected; signed-in users and link bearers with a signed-in companion keep any key. The new option `sharedScope: 'any'` (default `'random'`) takes any key, as before. Clients of anonymous or share-link routes that send other keys must switch to `crypto.randomUUID()` or the route must set `sharedScope: 'any'`.

`@spinajs/idempotency`: `IdempotencyService.peek(scope, key)` reads a scoped key without claiming it, at database time; `idempotencyPrincipal(principal)` and `isRestrictedPrincipal(principal)` build the principal part of a scope; `isSharedScope(principal)` tells whether a principal shares its key space with other callers, and `isRandomKey(key)` whether a key is a UUID of version 4 or 7. The package now depends on `@spinajs/principal`.
