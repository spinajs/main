`DomainReplayService` implements `ReplayService`: it loads a recording from the `RecordingSource` of its domain, replays it with the domain's `Replayer` over the `StateView` of the registered `StateSource`, and applies the verdict rules (`verdictOf`). Limits come from the new configuration section `replay.*` with per-domain overrides `replay.domains.<domain>.*` (`replayLimits`); every key has a ceiling, so a value the history or the timer would refuse fails the boot. A replay past a domain's `concurrency` throws `ReplayBusy`, a source throws `RecordingUnreadable` for a recording it cannot read back, a read of the view that failed decides the replay even when the replayer answers anyway, an answer given while a read of the view still runs is `unreplayable`, the timeout and the caller's abort end the call whatever the replayer does while its concurrency slot stays taken until the replayer settles, a recording dated past this clock plus `clockSkewMs` is `unreplayable`, `clockSkewMs` and `commitLagMs` are at least 1, and `ReplayService.replay` takes `maxChanges` beside `signal`.
