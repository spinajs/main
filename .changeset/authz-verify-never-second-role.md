`spinajs authz:verify` can fail after this upgrade where it passed before. `never(<holder>, <permission>)` no longer answers `holds (exact)` for a holder whose members reach the permission through a relation that a role earlier in sort order also holds, or through the second of two relations of one type held by that earlier role. With `role:auditor` and `role:reader` both on `doc#viewer`, `never({ holder: 'role:reader', permission: 'doc:read' })` answered `holds (exact)` although every named member of `doc#viewer` reads the document; with `role:auditor` on `doc#viewer` and `doc#commenter` and `read = viewer + commenter`, `never({ holder: 'doc#commenter', permission: 'doc:read' })` did the same. Both now fail with `AZV001` (exit 1 under the default `--fail-on error`) and a counterexample naming the member's tuple (`doc:d1#viewer@user:u1`). Where the permission is not monotone (a deny, an intersection, a caveat), such an assertion now goes to the bounded engine instead of answering `holds (exact)`; without `z3-solver` it is reported as `AZV090`, a warning, which fails `--fail-on warning` and, with `--require-solver`, the default gate. IRs compiled by `rbacToIR` give each role its own relation and are not affected; hand-written IRs are. If the role is meant to have the permission, drop the assertion or move the role to a relation of its own; otherwise take the permission away from the shared relation.
