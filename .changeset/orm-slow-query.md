Slow-query capture. `SlowQueryMiddleware` (a `QueryMiddleware` at order 2000) sends every statement slower than `db.Explain.SlowQueryMs` to all registered `SlowQuerySink`s as a `SlowQueryRecord` (sanitized SQL, fingerprint, driver-measured duration, trace and span ids). It runs after the result has returned, and only when `db.Explain.Enabled` is set (default off: no statement, binding or timestamp is kept; `reload()` re-reads the configuration). At most 100 captures are pending at once; more are dropped and counted in the log. For a single read it attaches the plan from the new optional `OrmDriver.explain()`, on the connection that ran the statement, once per fingerprint and minute, shared between overlapping captures and limited to `db.Explain.MaxPerMinute` (default 10) EXPLAIN calls. A plan is remembered per connection, and a statement that holds a `;` before its end, a comment, a `$`, a `#` or a backslash is recorded without a plan. Bindings, and the values a database prints inside a plan, are kept only with `db.Explain.CaptureBindings`. `LogSlowQuerySink` writes a warning on the `orm-slow-query` logger.
