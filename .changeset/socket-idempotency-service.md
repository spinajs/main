---
"@spinajs/http-socket": minor
---

Idempotent socket commands now keep their replies through `@spinajs/idempotency`. `http.socket.idempotency.service: 'MemoryIdempotencyStore'` (the shipped default, unchanged) now names the memory store of `@spinajs/idempotency`; `OrmIdempotencyStore` shares the keys between instances, and `service: null` binds sockets to the store of `IdempotencyService` (`idempotency.store`). A running claim is a lease (`http.socket.idempotency.lease`, or `idempotency.lease` when it is not set): a key left claimed by a shutdown abort is taken over by another instance once the lease ends, instead of refusing every resend for good, and a resend meanwhile gets the rest of the lease as its retry hint. A claim that loses a database lock or serialization conflict (`IdempotencyClaimConflict`) answers the retryable `unavailable` through the new `IdempotencyClaimConflictMapping`. The default memory store's `maxEntries` cap now evicts expired keys first and then the oldest completion, instead of the least recently used key. A handler whose lease is lost is aborted with `unavailable`, and a `crash: true` error keeps its key claimed until the lease ends. Binary parts of a reply are stored base64-encoded beside it, so they replay as binary. The 3.x `IdempotencyStore` and `MemoryIdempotencyStore` exports are deprecated and still work: a store extending them is wrapped by the new `fromLegacySocketStore()` and keeps the 3.x key format and semantics. Since http-socket now depends on `@spinajs/idempotency`, a socket app with a database gets the `__idempotency` table and its migration.
