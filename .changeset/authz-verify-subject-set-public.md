"@spinajs/authz-verify": patch
---

A member reached through a subject set (`tenant#member@group:g#member`, at any depth, cycles included) now counts as a member of the outer relation in every assertion. Before, a public grant behind such a set (`group#member@user:*`) was excused by `isolated('tenant', { except: ['tenant#member'] })` and by the bounded `onlyVia('tenant:enter', ['tenant#member'])`, and the bounded `never(tenant#member | role:member | public:tenant#member, ...)` missed every member behind the set, named or public; the exact `never(group#member, ...)` did not count the group's members for a permission reached through the set. Such a policy passed `spinajs authz:verify` and now fails it with `AZV001` (exit 1, under `--fail-on error` too) and a counterexample naming the subject-set tuple and the tuple behind it. If public access through the set is intended, list a public selector beside the relation (`except: ['tenant#member', 'public:tenant#member']`, or `'public'`); otherwise remove the wildcard from the set. A subject set that cannot hold a wildcard stays excused by its relation. `permissionMatrix` counts the same paths in the cells of the inner relation's holders.
