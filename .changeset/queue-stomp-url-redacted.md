Security: `StompQueueClient` wrote its broker URL (`host`) into the application's log as configured, so a credential in the URL (`wss://user:secret@broker/...`, `?token=...`) reached every log target of the application: at `info` in `Connecting to STOMP queue at ...` once per `resolve()`, at `trace` (or `debug` with `debug: true`) in the stompjs line `Connection closed to ...` on every close, so on every failed reconnect, and in the message of the error `resolve()` rejects with when the first connect fails, whose cause was the raw `ws` error event holding the socket, its URL and the upgrade request with the userinfo as an `Authorization: Basic` header. The stompjs `trace` lines also printed the `CONNECT` frame with the `passcode` header in clear on every connect and reconnect. The log lines and errors now print the broker URL without userinfo, query and fragment (`ws://broker:61614/stomp`, a path segment that may be a token as `***`, and `<not a URL>` for a value that does not parse), the `CONNECT` frame with `passcode:***`, and the cause of a rejection is an `Error` with only the socket failure's `name`, redacted `message` and `code` (for a broker `ERROR` frame: its `message` header, with the frame's text in `body`), not the `ws` event or the stompjs frame. A refused connection (`ECONNREFUSED`), a connection timeout and a broker `ERROR` frame (its `message` header, as before) still read differently. If a credential was ever part of `host`, or a `passcode` was logged at `trace` or `debug`, treat it as exposed and rotate it, and purge or shorten the retention of logs that hold it; put credentials in `login` / `password` (or a `credentialProvider`) instead of the URL.
