`.acrossTenants(reason)` on select, update and delete builders of a tenant model runs that one statement over the rows of every tenant; `acrossTenantsReason(builder)` reads the reason back. The middleware now also refuses an update that writes the tenant column, in every scope (`InvalidOperation`), and inside a tenant an update with no condition (`InvalidOperation`), an upsert on a tenant model (`NotSupported`) and, in a select of a model that is no tenant model, `populate()` of a to-one relation that joins a tenant table at any depth (`NotSupported`). On a connection whose tenant models declare row level security, one transaction keeps one tenant scope: a guard registered with `onTenantScopeChange` of `@spinajs/tenancy` refuses a change of scope while such a transaction is open, and a statement on a tenant table that runs in another scope than the first such statement of its transaction is refused too, both with `TenantSwitchDenied`. `carriesTenantSettings(driver)` tells whether a connection is one; `stopTransactionScopeGuard()` removes the guard.
