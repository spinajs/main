`Audit` on a trail with `onFailure: 'reject'` confirms an `attempt` event before the policy gate and the action run, and records the outcome under the same `data.invocation` once the response finished. When the attempt cannot be recorded the request is answered 503 (`AuditUnavailable`, registered for `AuditWriteFailed`) and nothing of the route runs; a trail the audit service refuses as misconfigured answers 500. `audit.http.rules` (`{ match, action, trail }`, `match` as `<METHOD or *> <path pattern>`) give matching routes their own action and trail where `Audit` is mounted; a rule that sets neither `action` nor `trail` fails the boot. `matchesRoute` is exported. A misspelt key under `audit.http` or in a rule now fails the boot instead of being dropped silently by the configuration check; for `audit.http` itself this closes a gap of the package's first version. A `HEAD` request to an audited `GET` route is audited: `methods` judges a request by its route's method.
