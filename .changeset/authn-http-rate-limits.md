The anonymous routes are rate limited by default: `POST /auth/login`, `/auth/code/login`, `/auth/keys/login`, `/auth/passkeys/login` and `/auth/keys/challenge` 30 a minute per client address, `/auth/passkeys/challenge` 30 a minute (a pending session counts on its own hashed handle), `/auth/code/request`, `/auth/password/reset-request` and `/auth/password/reset` 10 per 10 minutes, `/auth/verify` 20 per 10 minutes, `/auth/register` 10 an hour. Each is a `@RateLimit` in the `'outer'` stage, ahead of `@BotCheck` and the IP throttle gate, and answers what the route's IP throttle answers (the uniform 401 login failure with `Retry-After` on credential routes, the route's 429 `throttled` elsewhere), with no `X-RateLimit-*` headers. `authn.http.rateLimit.<name>` overrides the numbers (`{ limit, windowSeconds, by }` merged over the default) or takes a limit off with `false`; the schema checks the names and values. New exports: `AUTHN_HTTP_RATE_LIMITS`, `AUTHN_HTTP_RATE_LIMITED_ROUTES`, `rateLimitSchema`, `AuthnRateLimit`, `pendingSessionKey`.
