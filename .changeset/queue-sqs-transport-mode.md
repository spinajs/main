'@spinajs/queue-sqs-transport': minor
---

New connection option `retryMode: 'transport'` executes `@spinajs/queue`'s delivery decision: retries are re-sent with a delay and `x-retry-count` (FIFO: a visibility extension of at most 11 hours, with `x-retry-count` taken from the receive count), dead letters go to the routing / connection dead-letter queue URL with `x-error` and `x-original-destination` (FIFO: in the group the message was received in), unparseable bodies are dead-lettered, a failed event is deleted unless its route says `onEventFailure: 'retry'`. A `NonRetryableError` or `PermanentJobFailure` job is dead-lettered on its first failure. A FIFO queue with a redrive policy needs `maxReceiveCount > maxRetries + 1` in transport mode: `subscribe()` throws `InvalidArgument` otherwise, so `consume()` fails at boot with an error that names the queue, the count and the retries. A FIFO queue without a redrive policy is accepted; one whose policy cannot be read (no `sqs:GetQueueAttributes`) is accepted with a warning and runs unchecked. A job whose own `RetryCount` is above what a checked queue's `maxReceiveCount` holds is decided with `maxReceiveCount - 2` retries and dead-lettered by the transport, with its headers, and an error is logged. On FIFO a `RedeliverLater` and a redelivery after a crash each use one attempt. `'redrive'` stays the 4.x default. An invalid `retryMode` rejects `resolve()`. Transport mode sends from the consume side: it needs `sqs:SendMessage` on the consumed queue and on the dead-letter queue, and `sqs:ChangeMessageVisibility` on a FIFO queue. In both modes a `crash: true` error leaves the message as it is. Protected API: `executeDecision`, `handleUnparseable`, `handleFailure`, `attemptOf`, `receiveCountOf`, `RetryMode`, `readRetryMode`, `checkFifoRedrive`, `FifoReceiveLimits`, `WarnedRetryCaps`, `withinReceiveLimit`, `changeVisibility` and `redrivePolicyOf` are new.
