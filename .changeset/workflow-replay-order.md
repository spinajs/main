A workflow replay keeps the interleaving of concurrent branches. Each step row records how many results the workflow had received when the step was called (`Meta.after`) and the order its result was committed in (`Meta.order`, taken under the instance row lock). A replay hands recorded results back in that order, one per event-loop turn, each only once the calls that preceded it have been made again; live results that finish meanwhile wait until the recorded ones are delivered. Same-name steps across concurrent branches are replay-safe when the code between them awaits only workflow operations; otherwise per-item step names (`save:${id}`) are required, and with them non-durable awaits between steps are safe. A replay fails with `NonDeterminismError` when a new step appears where the journal recorded another, when a repeated step name is called at another position or after another number of results, or, through a `workflow.leaseTtl` timer on the injected `Clock`, when it waits for a recorded step that is never asked for. Waiting calls are settled as soon as the activation is interrupted. Journals without this metadata keep the previous lenient check.
