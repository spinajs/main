Every `isolated` assertion that holds, and every `never`, `onlyVia`, `implies` or `separated` that only the bounded engine answered `holds`, now prints the warning `AZV007` instead of the info `AZV002`: `warn AZV007 never(role:other, tenant:enter)  holds only up to k=2 (not proven)` in place of `ok   AZV002 never(role:other, tenant:enter)  verified up to k=2`. Such an answer means no counterexample with at most k objects per type, not a proof. The default exit code does not change (`--fail-on error` passes it). A pipeline that runs `spinajs authz:verify --fail-on warning` now also fails on every bounded holds. No `--fail-on` value keeps its old gate exactly: `--fail-on error` passes the bounded holds, but it also stops failing on `AZV003` (undecided or opaque) and `AZV090` (no solver). The new `--fail-on bounded` fails on an error or an `AZV007` line and on no other warning: it passes `AZV003` and `AZV090`. A gate that passes only on proofs runs `--fail-on warning`. A holds of the exact engine stays `AZV002` (`holds (exact)`). The `json` and `github` outputs carry `AZV007` with severity `warning`, in the shape of every other diagnostic; the `json` summary keeps its fields and counts it under `warning`.
