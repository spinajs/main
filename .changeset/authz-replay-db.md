`@spinajs/authz-orm/replay` registers `DbDecisionReplay` as the `DecisionReplay`: it reads a record of `authz_decision_log` (`readDecisionRecord`, re-exported here), the policy version stored under its hash and the authz changelog, all on the primary, and replays it with `replayDecision` of `@spinajs/authz-ir`, through every registered `HistoryContributors` (one per changelog kind) and `StoredPolicySplicers`. A row the log cannot read back as a record is `unreplayable` (`record`), an unknown id answers `undefined`. Limits come from `authz.replay` (`REPLAY_CONFIGURATION_SCHEMA`: `maxChanges` 50,000 up to 500,000, `maxKeys` 5,000, `maxDepth` 8, `maxCalls` 10,000, `timeoutMs` 15,000, `concurrency` 2, `clockSkewMs` 1,000, `commitLagMs` 5,000); a replay past `concurrency` is refused with `ReplayBusy`, now exported by `@spinajs/authz` beside `DecisionReplay`, and a replay inside a transaction open on the authz connection is refused with an `AuthzConfigError` (its changelog reads would see the transaction's snapshot while the stores' rows stay fresh). `ReplayTreeNode` declares `tupleContext`, the parameter paths a conditional tuple's context supplied to a caveat node. `readPolicyVersion` now reads the primary. A column read whose row was deleted, or whose column was cleared, outside the changelog after the decision is now a gap: the column contributor names the key even without a row.
