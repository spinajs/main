# `@spinajs/replay`

The domain-free core of replay: would a logged computation answer the same if it ran again at the instant it was made.

- `stateAt(t, input)`: the state of one keyed record at an instant, from its row now and its changes in a history stream, or a gap that names why it cannot be told. `coverageGap` and `sameImage` are the two checks it is built from.
- `ReplayChange`: one change of one key (`entity`, `key`, `op`, `image`, `before`, `at`, `seq`, `traceId`), the shape every history stream is read as.
- `ReplayHistory`: the bounded scan of a stream from the backward edge of a recording's window on, read through a `ChangeReader` (`afterInstant`, `latest`, `coveredSince`); `ReplayBudgetExceeded` when it reads more than its limits allow. A change a pass read new on a page after the first is held back until a later pass, so a key does not show a later change without an earlier one committed within the window's backward side. While a pass runs and after one failed, every read throws until a pass completes. A limit that is not a finite number in its range, and a reader whose pages do not rise above their cursor, are refused.
- `@spinajs/replay/testing`: `MemoryChangeReader`, a `ChangeReader` in memory for tests, with late commits and a hook before every read.
- The contracts of a replayable domain: `Recording` and `RecordingSource` (what ran), `ArtifactSource` (the versions it ran on), `StateSource` and `StateView` (state at the instant), `Replayer` (the domain's own evaluation), `ReplayReport` and `ReplayService`.

A `ReplayOutcome` whose `outcome` is other than `'unknown'` asserts that no input listed in `missing` could change it; a replayer that cannot assert that returns `'unknown'`.

`sameImage` compares two texts of a JSON object or array by value, with orm-history's rule for numbers a double cannot keep; a scalar text compares exactly. This is wider than history, which compares by value only in a column it knows as JSON: a plain text column holding object or array text, and a string nested in a JSON value, compare by value here too. It can only hide a write made outside the history.

- `DomainReplayService`, the registered `ReplayService`: `replay(domain, id, { signal, maxChanges })` loads the recording from the `RecordingSource` of its domain, gives the domain's `Replayer` the `StateView` of the process's `StateSource` at the recording's instant, applies the verdict rules (`verdictOf`), and refuses a replay past the domain's `concurrency` with `ReplayBusy`. A source throws `RecordingUnreadable` for a recording it cannot read back. A history budget or the timeout makes the replay `unreplayable`; any other failure of a read rejects the replay, also when the replayer caught a failed read of the view and answered anyway. A replayer that answers while a read of the view still runs gets `unreplayable` (`input: 'state'`). The timeout and the caller's abort end the call at once, whatever the replayer does: its work goes on in the background, against a view that rejects every read issued once the replay has ended and a signal that is aborted then; a synchronous loop cannot be stopped in-process. A replayer must honour its signal: its domain's concurrency slot stays taken until the replayer's promise settles, so one that never settles holds its slot forever, and one that floats a read of the view without a `catch` raises an unhandled rejection. An abort while the recording loads is seen when the load returns. Reads a replayer makes through its own `ReplayHistory` are not watched: the replayer lets their failures escape or answers `'unknown'`. A recording whose instant is later than this process's clock plus `clockSkewMs` is `unreplayable` (`input: 'record'`); a recorder a few seconds ahead, replayed hours later, cannot be told.
- Configuration `replay.*`: `maxChanges` (50,000, at most 500,000), `maxKeys` (5,000), `maxDepth` (8), `maxCalls` (10,000), `timeoutMs` (15,000, at most 2,147,483,647), `concurrency` (2), `clockSkewMs` (1,000, at least 1, at most 2,147,483,647), `commitLagMs` (5,000, at least 1, at most 2,147,483,647); the other keys at most `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER`. Each key can be set for one domain under `replay.domains.<domain>.*`. An unknown key or a value out of bounds fails the boot; `replayLimits(domain)` reads the limits and refuses the same with `InvalidOperation` where the configuration resolved before this package was imported. `replayWindow(recording, limits)` gives the ambiguity window.

The package reads no database; it depends on `@spinajs/di`, `@spinajs/exceptions` and `@spinajs/configuration-common` only. The history of ORM models and each domain are added by other packages.
